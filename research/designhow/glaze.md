## Agent procedure

Refund procedure

Runs when a customer asks for their money back.

1. Find the order with /lookup\_order.
2. If it is flagged for fraud, hand off to Trust and safety and stop.
3. Check the window in returns-policy.md and offer an exchange first.
4. Refund with /refund. Over $500, ask Support lead first.
