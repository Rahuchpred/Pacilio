# Anti-slop

Pacioli follows Glaze's anti-slop rules from design.how (research/designhow/glaze-anti-slop.md) and adds its own.

## Look
| Avoid | Instead |
|---|---|
| Gradients, blobs, grids, glow, blur | Ink and paper |
| A shadow to lift a card or a menu | Hairlines for cards, a 1px ring for overlays |
| A row of equal stat cards | A sentence or a table |
| Icons in tinted tiles | The heading alone |
| A colored bar on one edge | A pressed wash (`--color-action-decide-soft`) on the chosen row |
| A small round dot for a status | A glyph and a word |
| A card around everything, a card in a card | Space between groups, a card for the one thing acted on |

## Words
| Avoid | Instead |
|---|---|
| A small label above a title | The heading alone |
| Capitals and spaced letters | Sentence case |
| Emoji | Words and the icon set |
| Seamless, effortless, supercharge, AI-powered | What happened and when |
| Mono for labels and numbers | Sans with tabular figures. Mono only for IDs, keys, paths and code, at 0.8 |
| John Doe and 1,234 | Believable names and figures |

## Behaviour
| Avoid | Instead |
|---|---|
| Shimmer or a blinking cursor on streamed text | The text as it arrives |
| Content that fades in on scroll | Content that is there |

## Pacioli's own
- One accent per screen: the decide button. If two things are colored, one is wrong.
- No number without a source. No value without an actor.
- Never agree with a counterparty without a source.
