# Anti-slop

Settle never:
- uses gradients, glows or sparkle icons to mean AI. The agent is black and white like the ledger.
- uses a second accent color. If two things are colored, one of them is wrong.
- shows a number without a source.
- puts shadows on cards. Hairlines and surface steps only.
- centers marketing layouts inside the product.
- hides who did something. Every row has an actor.
- agrees with a counterparty without a source. It answers with citations or escalates.
