# Voice

- Name the person, the amount and the time. "Kept the $4,200 charge. M. Carter, 11:52 pm."
- Agent work is past tense and specific. "Matched 1,912 scans to the ticketing report."
- Ask a direct yes or no question only when a person must answer. Put the amount in the question.
- Say what happens if nobody answers. "Nothing posts until you answer."
- No exclamation marks, no emoji, no "AI-powered", no "Are you sure?".
- Buttons say what happens: "Keep the charge", "Cap it at $3,500", "Ask the tour manager".
